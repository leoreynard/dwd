Pay Water Bill via Maya

1
Open Maya App
Launch the Maya app on your device.
2
Navigate to Bills
Go to the 'Bills' section in the app.
3
Select Water Utilities
Search 'Dalaguete Water District'
4
Enter Details
Fill in your account number and amount.
5
Confirm Payment
Review details and confirm the payment.

Pay Water Bill via Gcash

1
Open GCash App
Launch the GCash app on your device.
2
Navigate to Bills
Go to the 'Pay Bills' section.
3
Select Water Utilities
Search 'Dalaguete Water District'
4
Enter Details
Enter the necessary account number and amount.
5
Confirm Payment
Review and confirm your water bill payment.
