Pay Water Bill via GCash

Step 1

Open your GCash app and log in to your account.

Step 2

On the home screen, tap on "Bills".

Step 3

Select or Tap "Water Utilities"

Step 4

Select or Tap "Dalaguete Water District"

Step 5

"Enter the necessary details: your amount, account name, account number, and email address (optional)."

Step 6

You will receive a confirmation message after a successful payment.
