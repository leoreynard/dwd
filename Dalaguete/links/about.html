About Dalaguete Water District

Committed to providing clean and sustainable water solutions.

We continuously improve infrastructure to ensure efficient water management.

OUR MISSION

DWD commits to develop and improve potable water supplies for environmental water management. Increase water reliability and ensure concessionaire's satisfaction and strive to attain financial viability through prudent use of our financial resources..

OUR VISION

To be the leading water district in the region. well-managed Water District providing quality service and sustainable potable water supply to the Municipality of Dalaguete. The district perceives the necessity to protect, reforest and rehabilitate its watersheds for continuous and abundant supply of water

OUR GOALS

Our constant goal is to provide adequate, affordable and potable supply of drinking water and provide efficient services in partnership with our concessionaire and the community.

OUR HISTORY

The Dalaguete Water District (DWD) was officially established on April 19, 1990, following the issuance of Certificate of Conformance No. 436 by the Local Water Utilities Administration (LWUA).

As a government-owned and controlled corporation, DWD operates under the authority of Presidential Decree No. 198, as amended. In 2012, the Department of Budget and Management categorized DWD as a Category "C" Water District, reflecting its operational scale and capacity.

As of December 31, 2018, DWD employed a total of 25 personnel, comprising 17 regular staff, five casual employees, and three job order personnel, under the leadership of Acting General Manager Anthony C. Osorio.

DWD is committed to providing quality service and sustainable potable water supply to the Municipality of Dalaguete. Recognizing the importance of environmental stewardship, the district actively engages in protecting, reforesting, and rehabilitating its watersheds to ensure a continuous and abundant water supply.

To enhance its services, DWD participates in initiatives like the WaterWorX project, a collaboration aimed at improving the performance of water utilities in the Philippines through capacity building and technical assistance.

Through these efforts, DWD strives to deliver adequate, affordable, and potable water to its concessionaires, reflecting its dedication to public service and community partnership.
